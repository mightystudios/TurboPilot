# Welcome to TurboPilot

**Modern AI. Classic interface. Your model, your workspace, your rules.**

Choose **Session > Begin Session** to connect GitHub Copilot or an
OpenAI-compatible cloud or local model.

1. Select the workspace the model may use.
2. Choose a provider, model, reasoning effort, and mode.
3. Write a prompt and press `Ctrl+Enter` or choose **Send**.

- Add files with the `+0` button, drag and drop, or pasted images.
- Read formatted replies in **Rendered** and exact transcript text in **Raw**.
- Review tool activity, permission requests, plans, and workspace changes.
- Choose **Help > Show Help** for the complete guide in a separate window you
  can keep open while you work.

No session is active yet. Start or resume one when you are ready.
